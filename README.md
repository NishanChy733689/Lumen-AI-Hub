🤖 What is Lumen AI Hub?

Lumen AI Hub is an all-in-one platform for discovering, downloading, managing, and running Ollama AI models. It provides a simple web interface that lets users access local AI from both computers and mobile devices.

🚀 What Can It Do?

⬇️ Browse and download AI models

💬 Chat with different AI models

🔄 Switch models instantly

📱 Access AI from any device on your LAN

🛠️ Use AI models with supported tool functions

⚙️ Manage all models from one dashboard

🌟 Why Choose Lumen AI Hub?
No terminal commands required
Simple and user-friendly interface
One platform for all your AI models
Multi-device access over a local network
Fast deployment in homes, schools, and offices
📍 Where Can It Be Used?

🏫 Education – AI learning assistant for classrooms and labs.

🏢 Workplaces – Shared AI assistant for teams and productivity.

🏠 Home – A personal AI server for the whole family.

🔬 Research – Quickly test and compare different AI models.
